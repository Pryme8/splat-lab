Splat Lab: training benchmark

Trains each primitive type at the app's default sizes against a procedural torus-knot teacher and reports steps per second and loss. Defaults: 400,000 teacher surfels, 200,000 Gaussians, 120,000 triangles (opaque finish), 60,000 convexes, 3,000 steps, 256², 64 views. Query parameters override them, for example ?types=1&steps=500&n1=40000 (keys: types steps res views teacher n0 n1 n2). Profile times every compute pass of a training step with timestamp queries and reports GPU milliseconds per kernel (extra key: iters).
